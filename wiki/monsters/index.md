# Monsters

Every monster that appears in the game: its stats, where it spawns and what it drops. A monster page counts drops in "slots of 30", explained on [[rules/drops|Drops]]; spawn points are explained on [[rules/monster-spawns|Monster spawns]] and how monsters fight on [[rules/monster-behaviour|Monster behaviour]].

The XP column is the monster's experience value before the level difference and party rules on [[rules/experience|Experience]].
