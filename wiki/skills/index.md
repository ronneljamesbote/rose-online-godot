# Skills

Every skill in the game, with its numbers at each level: what it needs, what it costs, its range, cooldown, power and effects. How skills are learnt, cast and how their damage is worked out is on [[rules/skills|Skills]]; buffs and debuffs are on [[rules/status-effects|Status effects]].

The list is sorted by internal ID, which groups skills of the same job together. Click a column title to sort by it.
