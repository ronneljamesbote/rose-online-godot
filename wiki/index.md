Everything about the game in one place: how combat and experience work, every skill with
its numbers per level, where monsters live and what they drop, where to find each NPC and
what they say, every quest from start to end, and every item.

This wiki is also the game's specification. What a page says is what the game does, and
changing the game starts with changing its page. See [[rules/how-this-wiki-works|How this wiki works]].
