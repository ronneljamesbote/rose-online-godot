# Riverford Hat

A warden's hat from Riverford Post, sewn for Visitors who keep watch on the plain. Part of the Riverford Visitor set, given by the people of the Adventurer's Plain to Visitors who helped them. Any job can wear it.

A reward for [[quests/2207-tracks-to-the-ford|Tracks to the Ford]]. It isn't sold or dropped anywhere.

## Changed from iROSE

A new item of the Adventurer's Plain redesign, using the model and icon of [[items/head/303-red-beret|Red Beret]] with better stats, and no job or stat requirement.
