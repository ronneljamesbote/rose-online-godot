# Hovert's Letter

Hovert's final letter. It reads, "Pein, my dear grandson, I'm fighting the swarms of Aquas that are ruining the lake. But no matter how many I kill, there seems to be no end to them.  I have to wipe out the Aqua Captains first, but it may already be too late. If I fail, please seek out a hero that can defeat these Aqua Captains in my place."
