# Riverford Gloves

Soft deerskin gloves with a river-blue cuff. Part of the Riverford Visitor set, given by the people of the Adventurer's Plain to Visitors who helped them. Any job can wear it.

A reward for [[quests/2203-bones-on-the-beach|Bones on the Beach]]. It isn't sold or dropped anywhere.

## Changed from iROSE

A new item of the Adventurer's Plain redesign, using the model and icon of [[items/hands/203-action-gloves|Action Gloves]] with better stats, and no job or stat requirement.
