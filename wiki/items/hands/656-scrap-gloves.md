# Scrap Gloves

Torn work gloves from the Ferrell Guild's scrap pile. Only good for practice: put them in storage, or take them apart for their cloth (see [[rules/crafting|Crafting]]). Worth next to nothing, and they can't be traded.

[[quests/2403-safe-keeping|Safe Keeping]] and [[quests/2409-nothing-goes-to-waste|Nothing Goes to Waste]] give a pair each. They aren't sold or dropped anywhere.
