# Riverford Boots

Light boots that dry fast after a ford crossing. Part of the Riverford Visitor set, given by the people of the Adventurer's Plain to Visitors who helped them. Any job can wear it.

A reward for [[quests/104-mysterious-emblem|Mysterious Emblem]]. It isn't sold or dropped anywhere.

## Changed from iROSE

A new item of the Adventurer's Plain redesign, using the model and icon of [[items/feet/203-shoes-of-iguje|Shoes of Iguje]] with better stats, and no job or stat requirement.
