# Riverford Coat

A travelling coat in Riverford colours, given for driving off the Jelly King. Part of the Riverford Visitor set, given by the people of the Adventurer's Plain to Visitors who helped them. Any job can wear it.

A reward for [[quests/2209-the-jelly-king|The Jelly King]]. It isn't sold or dropped anywhere.

## Changed from iROSE

A new item of the Adventurer's Plain redesign, using the model and icon of [[items/body/203-natalie-look|Natalie Look]] with better stats, and no job or stat requirement.
