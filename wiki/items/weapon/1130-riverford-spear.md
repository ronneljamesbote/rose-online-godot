# Riverford Spear

A ferry pole with an iron head, long enough to keep a Choropy at bay. A Riverford Visitor weapon from Warden Corvel's armoury. Any job can use it.

A reward you can pick when you finish [[quests/2205-ford-watch|Ford Watch]]. It isn't sold or dropped anywhere.
