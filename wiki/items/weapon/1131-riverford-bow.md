# Riverford Bow

A reed-wrapped short bow. Corvel gives a quiver of Wooden Arrows with it. A Riverford Visitor weapon from Warden Corvel's armoury. Any job can use it.

A reward you can pick when you finish [[quests/2205-ford-watch|Ford Watch]]. It isn't sold or dropped anywhere.
