# Riverford Gun

A sturdy little gun, oiled against the river damp. Corvel gives a pouch of Bean Bullets with it. A Riverford Visitor weapon from Warden Corvel's armoury. Any job can use it.

A reward you can pick when you finish [[quests/2205-ford-watch|Ford Watch]]. It isn't sold or dropped anywhere.
