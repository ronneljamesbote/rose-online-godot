# Sandwind Blade

A curved caravan-guard sword, light enough to swing all day. Brakka gives it to the soldiers of the Sandwind Caravan.

A reward you can pick when you finish [[quests/2113-the-sergeant-s-trial|The Sergeant's Trial]]. It isn't sold or dropped anywhere.

A new item of the Valley of Luxem Tower redesign, using the model and icon of [[items/weapon/4-khukuri|Khukuri]] with better quality, a 10 level requirement and no stat requirement.
