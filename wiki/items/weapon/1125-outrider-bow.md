# Outrider Bow

A long desert bow, strung for the caravan's scouts. Kesh gives it to new hawkers.

A reward you can pick when you finish [[quests/2115-the-outrider-s-trial|The Outrider's Trial]]. It isn't sold or dropped anywhere.
