# Practice Sword

A blunt sword from Raffle's practice rack in Zant, for learning to repair and refine. Any job can use it, it is worth next to nothing and it can't be traded.

[[quests/2406-mending-steel|Mending Steel]] gives one at 30% life, to have repaired; [[quests/2407-sharper-steel|Sharper Steel]] gives a new one to refine. It isn't sold or dropped anywhere.

## Changed from iROSE

A new item of the Zant city hub, using the model and icon of the [[items/weapon/1-wooden-sword|Wooden Sword]] with weaker stats.
