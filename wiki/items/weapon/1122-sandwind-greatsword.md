# Sandwind Greatsword

A heavy two-handed sword that has kept the Caravan Road open for years. Brakka gives it to the soldiers of the Sandwind Caravan.

A reward you can pick when you finish [[quests/2113-the-sergeant-s-trial|The Sergeant's Trial]]. It isn't sold or dropped anywhere.

## Changed from iROSE

A new item of the Valley of Luxem Tower redesign, using the model and icon of [[items/weapon/102-nameless-sword|Nameless Sword]] with better quality, a 10 level requirement and no stat requirement.
