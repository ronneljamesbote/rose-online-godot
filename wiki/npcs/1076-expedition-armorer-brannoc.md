# [Expedition Armorer] Brannoc

Stands at the Arumic Expedition Camp in [[zones/21-valley-of-luxem-tower|Valley of Luxem Tower]]. Sells one-handed and two-handed weapons, bows and combat weapons, a step up from the caravan smith (see [[rules/shops|Shops]]).

## Dialog

The NPC says the **last** message in a list whose condition passes; the lines under
it are your choices. See [[rules/npc-dialogs|NPC dialogs]].

- **NPC:** The expedition can't afford to lose people to the ruins. Take something that hits hard.
  - **You:** Let me see your goods.  → opens the shop
  - **You:** Goodbye.

After an answer the NPC offers "Something else." (back to the first list) and "Goodbye."
