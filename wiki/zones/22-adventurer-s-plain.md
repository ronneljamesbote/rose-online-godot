# Adventurer's Plain

**Every new character starts here**, at the zone's start spot (5760.45 m, 5166.60 m) on the
beach, 4 m in front of [[npcs/1031-visitor-guide-ronway|Visitor Guide Ronway]] and the
[[npcs/1030-visitor-guide-fairy-of-arua|Fairy of Arua]] (see [[rules/accounts|Accounts]]).
Moving the "start" marker in the zone's scene moves where new characters start. In iROSE they
start on [[zones/20-birth-island|Birth Island]] and come here when they leave it.

Positions are in metres. Each spawn point keeps up to "max alive" monsters (see
[[rules/monster-spawns|Monster spawns]]). Zone drops can come from any monster here (see
[[rules/drops|Drops]]).

A green coastal plain on the east coast of Junon. Two rivers come down from waterfalls in
the north and run south to the sea; the land west of them dries out toward the gates to the
[[zones/21-valley-of-luxem-tower|Valley of Luxem Tower]]. You arrive at Arua's Landing, the
pier village on the beach, and walk west, the monsters getting stronger as you go, to
Riverford Post at the bridges and on to the gates. Strangers in red cloaks camped at Bramble
Ford not long ago, and since they left the mushrooms rot, the seed in the fields is poisoned
and the Jelly King has grown fat in its hollow.

| Goblin Cave crossing | far south-west, over the bridge | — | Gate Guard Bram and the palisade before the Goblin Cave gate (levels 45 and up) |

One elite lives here: the [[monsters/203-jelly-king|Jelly King]] (level 13) in its hollow
west of Bramble Ford. It comes back 3 minutes after it falls. Butterflies, fireflies and pond
fish live on the plain too; they can't be targeted.

## Shops

- Arua's Landing: [[npcs/1034-smith-ronk|Ronk]] (Visitor weapons and clothes, repairs) and
  [[npcs/1035-little-street-vendor-pony|Pony]] (fruit), as in iROSE.
- Riverford Post: [[npcs/1043-ferrywoman-maeve|Maeve]] (food, medicines and return scrolls).

## Getting around

- Three gates, at the same places as in iROSE: two in the north-west to the
  [[zones/21-valley-of-luxem-tower|Valley of Luxem Tower]] and one in the far south-west to
  the [[zones/31-goblin-cave-b1|Goblin Cave]]. The arrival spots are the same too.
- The road runs from the landing west to Riverford Post, then north up to Gatehill and south
  to Bramble Ford. Two bridges at Riverford cross the rivers, and another crosses to the
  Goblin Cave gate.
- [[npcs/1043-ferrywoman-maeve|Maeve]] can make Riverford Post your save point.

## Revive spots

When you revive here you wake at the revive spot nearest to where you fell (see
[[rules/death|Death]]):

- (5748, 5182), Arua's Landing
- (5466, 5352), Riverford Post
- (5125, 5490), below the north-west Luxem gate
- (5345, 5480), below the northern Luxem gate
- (5190, 5148), by the Goblin Cave crossing

The chain gives the Riverford Visitor set, better than anything Visitors can buy and
wearable by any job: [[items/hands/655-riverford-gloves|gloves]] from Bones on the Beach,
[[items/feet/655-riverford-boots|boots]] from Myad (Mysterious Emblem), a weapon you pick
from Ford Watch ([[items/weapon/1129-riverford-sword|sword]],
[[items/weapon/1130-riverford-spear|spear]], [[items/weapon/1131-riverford-bow|bow]] with
arrows or [[items/weapon/1132-riverford-gun|gun]] with bullets), the
[[items/head/835-riverford-hat|hat]] from Tracks to the Ford and the
[[items/body/655-riverford-coat|coat]] from The Jelly King.

[[quests/2210-over-the-ridge|Over the Ridge]] ends at [[npcs/1054-caravan-master-dorran|Dorran]],
who starts the [[zones/21-valley-of-luxem-tower|Valley of Luxem Tower]] story with
[[quests/2101-dust-on-the-road|Dust on the Road]]. The job change is in that valley too, with
the job mentors at the Sandwind Caravan Camp.

The kill quests share their monsters with older quests (the Fairy of Arua's and Gray's
hunting quests and the Hawker job change quest): a kill counts for both.

Adventurer's Plain is edited in Godot: its spawn points, NPCs and start and revive spots are
markers in the zone's scene, and the server reads them from

## Changed from iROSE

- New characters start here instead of on Birth Island, and one Fairy of Arua (iROSE has
  her only on Birth Island) stands next to Ronway at the start.
- The plain is redesigned. The coast, the two rivers, the pier village, the three gates and
  their arrival spots and the start are as in iROSE; the rest is new: the ground's shape and
  colour inland (drying toward the gates), the welcome square, Riverford Post, Tibbs' farm,
  the Gatehill watchtower, the strangers' camp and the Jelly King's hollow, the spawn points
  (sorted by level from east to west instead of mixed, with the Jelly King as the one elite
  instead of a rare reinforcement), the butterflies, fireflies and fish, nine new NPCs, the
  quest chain with its Riverford Visitor set, and the revive spots (iROSE has five, all
  different). The minimap is redrawn to match.
- Myad's emblem quest ends at Warden Corvel instead of Karitte in the Valley of Luxem Tower,
  and Herbalist Linnea at the mushroom ring stands in for the clue you click in iROSE.
