# Zones

Every zone with its map: monster spawn points, NPCs and warp gates are drawn on it, and the zone's own drop table is listed below it. Map positions are in metres from the zone's top left corner. How zones, warp gates and save points work is on [[rules/zones-and-warps|Zones and warps]]; PvP zones are explained on [[rules/pvp|PvP]].
