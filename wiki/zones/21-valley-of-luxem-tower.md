# Valley of Luxem Tower

Positions are in metres. Each spawn point keeps up to "max alive" monsters; the
reinforcements show up once a point has been refilled many times (see
[[rules/monster-spawns|Monster spawns]]). Zone drops can come from any monster here (see
[[rules/drops|Drops]]).

A desert valley between the Adventurer's Plain and Zant. Luxem Tower stands on a raised mesa
in the north-east corner. A spring under the mesa feeds a river that runs south-west to an
oasis pond; past the oasis the old riverbed is a dry wadi that runs south-east into the
red-rock canyons. Somewhere under the valley, something is waking: the tower's crystal is
dimming, and the monsters are changing.

Two elite monsters live here: [[monsters/76-sandscar|Sandscar]] (level 21) roams the high
ground north of the ruins and comes back about every 10 minutes, and the
[[monsters/77-ruin-warden|Ruin Warden]] (level 22) guards the sealed vault.

## Shops

- Sandwind Caravan Camp: [[npcs/1074-caravan-smith-garro|Garro]] (weapons),
  [[npcs/1075-caravan-outfitter-nessa|Nessa]] (armour and clothes) and
  [[npcs/1057-caravan-trader-sella|Sella]] (medicines, return scrolls, materials, magic weapons).
- Arumic Expedition Camp: [[npcs/1076-expedition-armorer-brannoc|Brannoc]] (weapons),
  [[npcs/1077-expedition-tailor-yselle|Yselle]] (armour and clothes) and
  [[npcs/1065-quartermaster-hask|Hask]] (potions, return scrolls, repair tools, magic weapons).
  Their goods are a step up from the caravan's.
- Skill books for the four first jobs: [[npcs/1078-caravan-tutor-ilsa|Ilsa]] at the caravan camp and
  [[npcs/1079-expedition-instructor-corwin|Corwin]] at the expedition camp.

## Getting around

- Four gates, at the same places as in iROSE: to [[zones/1-canyon-city-of-zant|Zant]] in
  the north-west, to [[zones/23-breezy-hills|Breezy Hills]] on the East Terrace, and two to
  the [[zones/22-adventurer-s-plain|Adventurer's Plain]] in the south.
- Two bridges cross the river and one the wadi; a rope bridge links the ruins to the
  overlook on the western ridge.
- [[npcs/1058-expedition-leader-ardeth|Ardeth]]'s crane lowers you into the sealed vault
  once you have finished [[quests/2110-words-in-stone|Words in Stone]] (level 16 or more);
  [[npcs/1070-lift-keeper-gus|Gus]] in the vault brings you back up to the dig.
- [[npcs/1055-scout-mira|Mira]] at the caravan camp can make the camp your save point.

Camp and the dig spots are new.

The kill quests share their monsters with the job-change quests in this valley: a kill
counts for both.

Valley of Luxem Tower is edited in Godot: its spawn points, NPCs and start and revive spots
are markers in the zone's scene, and the server reads them from
`export/zones/21-valley-of-luxem-tower.toml`, which is exported from that scene.

## Changed from iROSE

The whole valley is redesigned. The size, the four gates and their arrival spots are the
same as in iROSE; everything else is new: the ground's shape and textures, the tower moved
to the north-east corner and made bigger, the river, oasis and wadi, the trees along the water, the two camps, the
ruins, the spawn points (levels 9–22 instead of 9–20), the two elite monsters, the sealed
vault, nineteen new NPCs and the quest chain. The minimap is redrawn to match, and its frame says Breezy Hills and Adventurer's Plain
(iROSE's minimap said "Hill of wind" and "Adventure Plane").
